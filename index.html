Flying lessons and trial flights on the Isle of Wight

Isle of Flight is a flying school based at Sandown Airport on the Isle of Wight. We offer flying lessons and trial flights for complete beginners as well as those who already hold a licence. Whether you want a one-off experience or to start training toward a National Private Pilot’s Licence, we keep things straightforward and enjoyable.

Our fleet ranges from modern enclosed Ikarus C42 microlights to a vintage Stampe biplane. The C42s are stable, well-equipped two-seat aircraft that make an excellent introduction to flying, while the Stampe offers a more traditional open-cockpit experience. All flights take place over the Isle of Wight coastline, with views of the Needles, Sandown Bay and the surrounding Solent.

Trial lessons are available in 30-minute, 60-minute and 90-minute options and make popular gift ideas on the Isle of Wight. You can book online, including at short notice when weather and availability allow. Many visitors choose a flying lesson as one of the more memorable things to do on the Isle of Wight, and aeroplane gift vouchers are a regular request for birthdays and special occasions.

We operate from Sandown Airport and focus on clear instruction, good aircraft and a relaxed atmosphere. If you’re looking for a flying school on the Isle of Wight, a trial flight, or simply want to see the island from the air, we can help.
